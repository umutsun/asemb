What are the penalties for late payment of corporate tax? 14:31

Late-Payment Penalties under UAE Corporate Tax

Late payment of corporate tax is penalised under Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, with the penalty schedule set by Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023[1][2]. Two layers apply:

  1. Monthly late-payment penalty — 14% per annum, applied monthly on the unpaid tax from the day after the payment deadline[2].
  2. Fixed administrative penalties — for related failures such as late registration or late filing of the tax return, per the schedule annexed to Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023[2].
  3. Voluntary disclosure — submitting a disclosure before an audit notice can reduce the applicable penalties under the Tax Procedures Law[3].

Penalties accrue on the net unpaid amount; settling the principal stops further monthly accrual[1].
